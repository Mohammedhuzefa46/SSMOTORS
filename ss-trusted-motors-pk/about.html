Who We Are

Hyderabad's Most
Trusted Dealer

Founded in 2012, SS Trusted Motors was born from a simple belief: buying a pre-owned car should be as transparent and exciting as buying a new one. For over a decade, we've served thousands of happy customers across Hyderabad and Telangana.

Our inventory spans every segment — from German luxury (BMW, Mercedes, Audi, Volvo) to family SUVs, refined sedans, city hatchbacks, and a rare vintage collection that includes Ambassadors, Fiat Padminis, WWII Jeeps, and American muscle cars.

We were the first dealership in Telangana to introduce QR-based car profiles — letting you scan, know, and decide before you even speak to a salesperson.
